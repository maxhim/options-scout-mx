How to read the brief

Signal strength, 0–100How well the trend, money flowing into options, unusual volume and yesterday’s flow that stuck as open interest agree. Stocks enter the brief from 55, options only from 70 (highest conviction), intraday alerts from 65. Strength is halved when an idea fights relative strength (a short on a stock stronger than the market) or chases a move that already ran far: a short after a crash, a long after a spike (“snap-back risk”).

Stock target and stopThe target is the expected move over the idea’s horizon, using the higher of implied (IV30) and 20-day realized volatility. The stop is half of that, but never closer than 1 ATR. Ideas above 60% annual volatility are flagged ⚠.

OptionsTake-profit +100% of premium, stop −50%, exit no later than the day before expiry. Chance of profit is the Black–Scholes probability of finishing beyond break-even.

What the signals cannot knowRising open interest does not show who bought and who sold. Trust the track record only after 50–100 closed ideas. Analytics, not investment advice.
